# KaloriiAI — Modern Clean Health Design System & PRD ## 1. Visual Identity & Aesthetic: Modern Clean Health A sophisticated, modern health & nutrition tracker inspired by Apple Health, Whoop, and Oura. Focused on clarity, high data density without clutter, frictionless food logging, and inspiring feedback. - **Color Palette (Dark Mode First with Light Mode Support):** - Background Base: #0B0F17 (Deep OLED Midnight) - Card Surface: #151C28 with subtle 1px border #222F3E - Secondary Surface / Muted: #1E293B - Text Primary: #FFFFFF, Text Secondary: #94A3B8, Text Muted: #64748B - Primary Accent (Health / Energy): #10B981 (Emerald Green) - Calories Glow: #F97316 (Vibrant Coral / Sunset Orange) - Protein: #6366F1 (Electric Indigo) - Fats: #F59E0B (Warm Amber / Gold) - Carbs: #06B6D4 (Cyan / Sky) - Over-limit / Alert: #EF4444 (Crimson) - **Typography & Geometry:** - Font: Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter. Clean sans-serif. - Numbers: Bold tabular numerals (font-semibold tabular-nums). - Radii: 20px-24px for cards, 9999px for pill badges and primary buttons. - Elevation: Soft diffuse ambient glow around active rings and key metric cards. ## 2. Core Functional Requirements (Must Preserve 100% of KaloriiAI): 1. **Week Strip Calendar:** Horizontal 7-day selector showing completed ring dots for past days. 2. **Concentric / Circular KBJU Targets:** - Remaining calories hero counter with ring visualization. - Distinct progress bars/rings for Protein, Fats, and Carbs. 3. **Chronological Meals List:** - Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack sections. - Shows calories, macros breakdown, timestamp, and quick add button. 4. **AI Vision Camera Food Recognition Modal:** - Captured dish preview photo. - Multi-ingredient detected tags with confidence ratings. - Adjustable weight/gram sliders with automatic total recalculation. 5. **Barcode Scanner & Portion Sheet:** - Live barcode camera view + recent products carousel. - Portion size picker with instant macro calculation. 6. **Analytics & Weight Progress:** - Calorie intake vs daily target bar charts. - Weight trendline with starting, current, and goal weight. 7. **Bottom Action Dock:** - Navigation: Today, Stats, Central [ + / 📷 AI Scan ], Meal Plans, Profile.
